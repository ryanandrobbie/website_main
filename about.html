Introducing Robots for Reading

We’re designing something new: a playful, research-informed approach to support early learning and connection between parents and children. Robots for Reading is a project that blends classic storytelling with next-gen interactivity— helping parents spark conversations, stories, and social-emotional learning through play.

Imagine a future where your child’s favorite storybook companion can grow with them, respond to their curiosity, and bring the joy of serve-and-return interactions into a digital space—all while keeping parents at the center.
